OUR STORY

Japanese convenience,
made fresh in Chelsea.

Onimart is a Japanese grocery and delicatessen bringing the convenience and flavors of Japan to the heart of Chelsea, New York City.

Our team prepares onigiri, sushi rolls, hand rolls and nigiri in-store every day using quality ingredients. From classic favorites to creative specialties, our focus is simple: fresh food that's easy to grab and enjoy.
